They are distributed under the Open Database License 1.0; attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors. The four teaching buildings listed in `campus.json` are retained even when a footprint falls just outside the park relation.

## Campus map source-grounded refinement (2026-11-27)

- Added three non-overlapping Overture Maps 2026-11-18 building footprints inside the campus park boundary. Existing OSM building geometries and OSM IDs remain the primary source where a footprint already exists.
- The additions were selected from a TerraLab extract of the narrow campus area [30.3405, 59.9915, 30.3475, 59.9965]. Only footprints whose centroid falls inside the existing OSM park boundary and whose bounding box does not substantially overlap an existing building were added.
- New footprints retain source provenance as Overture Maps; height and facade/roof attributes are used only when present. Overture building data combines OSM, Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints, Google Open Buildings, Esri Community Maps and other contributors. Licence and attribution details: https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/#buildings.
- Unknown-height buildings now remain flat, selectable footprints. Their height is not fabricated. Floor-count estimates are labelled as estimates in the building card.
- Removed algorithmic placement of hundreds of illustrative trees across green polygons. The viewer now displays only the seven individual tree points present in the current OSM scene. Their positions are sourced; canopy size and height are still illustrative because individual survey attributes are not mapped.
- Facade windows remain procedural visual guides, not verified window surveys or photogrammetry. Do not describe this scene as photorealistic.
- Dataset licence: Overture's Buildings data is distributed under ODbL 1.0 with constituent source credits; preserve © Overture Maps Foundation and © OpenStreetMap contributors attribution. See https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/.
