# Campus map data and media credits

- The park boundary, paths, water, woodland, trees, amenities, and building footprints come from OpenStreetMap relation 5622070 and the linked OSM objects. They are distributed under the Open Database License 1.0; attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors. The four teaching buildings listed in `campus.json` are retained even when a footprint falls just outside the park relation.
- The main building's approximate date and architectural period are shown with a link to [LAYRS: Петербург](https://stop-token.com/ru/lab/spb/). LAYRS documents that its data comes from multiple sources with separate licences; this project uses only the small, attributed facts shown in the building card and does not copy its city dataset or map engine.
- The aerial photograph `miniapp/assets/campus-main-building-aerial.jpg` is a Wikimedia Commons thumbnail of [“Санкт-Петербург, главный корпус Лестеха сверху (1).jpg”](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%D0%A1%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%BA%D1%82-%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B1%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B3,_%D0%B3%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B2%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B9_%D0%BA%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BF%D1%83%D1%81_%D0%9B%D0%B5%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B5%D1%85%D0%B0_%D1%81%D0%B2%D0%B5%D1%80%D1%85%D1%83_(1).jpg), by Nikolai Bulykin, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). It is an oblique aerial photograph used in the building information card, not a georeferenced map texture.
- The OSM footprint's tagged height, floor count, material, roof shape, and architectural style are displayed only when present in the OSM data. Untagged heights remain marked as illustrative.
